## Profile

**Xi'myr has been worshipped under many names; _Hemsut_ by the Egyptians, _Ori_ in Africa, _Bali_ in Asia, ect. Xi'myr is the deity of Time. They are in control of the [[Glass Desert]] in the Northwest Sector, and are the creator of the Fractured.**
