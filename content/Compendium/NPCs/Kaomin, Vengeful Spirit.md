## Profile

**Once a heroic Paladin of The Castle of Order, Kaomin broke his Oath when his daughter was to be executed for witchcraft. He failed to save her, but slaughtered the previous King, leaving King Gabriel in power.  Now Kaomin, Vengeful Spirit is the leader of the High Elves who are in control of the Gnome Grove in the Southwest Sector.**
