# Ability Score and Modifier

_Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 360. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_

A creature has six ability scores—Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma—each of which has a corresponding modifier. Add the modifier when you make a [D20 Test](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/d20-test-xphb.md) with the corresponding ability or when a rule asks you to do so.
