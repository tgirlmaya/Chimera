# Charm of Animal Conjuring

This Charm allows you to cast [Conjure Animals](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/spells/conjure-animals-xphb.md). Once used three times, the Charm vanishes from you.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 99_
