# Charm of Darkvision

This Charm allows you to cast [Darkvision](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/spells/darkvision-xphb.md). Once used three times, the Charm vanishes from you.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 99_
