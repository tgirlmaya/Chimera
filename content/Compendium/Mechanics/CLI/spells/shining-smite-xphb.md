# Shining Smite

_2nd-level, Transmutation_

- **Casting time:** 1 Bonus Action, which you take immediately after hitting a creature with a Melee weapon or an [Unarmed Strike](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/unarmed-strike-xphb.md)
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

The target hit by the strike takes an extra `2d6` Radiant damage from the attack. Until the spell ends, the target sheds [Bright Light](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/bright-light-xphb.md) in a 5-foot radius, attack rolls against it have [Advantage](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/advantage-xphb.md), and it can't benefit from the [Invisible](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/conditions.md#Invisible) condition.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The damage increases by `1d6` for each spell slot level above 2.

**Classes**: [Paladin](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/lists/list-spells-classes-paladin.md)

_Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 316. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_
