# Hellish Rebuke

_1st-level, Evocation_

- **Casting time:** 1 Reaction, which you take in response to taking damage from a creature that you can see within 60 feet of yourself
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

The creature that damaged you is momentarily surrounded by green flames. It makes a Dexterity saving throw, taking `2d10` Fire damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The damage increases by `1d10` for each spell slot level above 1.

**Classes**: [Warlock](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/lists/list-spells-classes-warlock.md)

_Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 284. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_
