# Purify Food and Drink

_1st-level, Transmutation (ritual)_

- **Casting time:** 1 Action unless cast as a ritual
- **Range:** 10 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You remove poison and rot from nonmagical food and drink in a 5-foot-radius [Sphere](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/sphere-area-of-effect-xphb.md) centered on a point within range.

**Classes**: [Bard (College of Lore)](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/lists/list-spells-classes-college-of-lore-xphb.md "subclass=XPHB;class=XPHB"); [Bard](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/lists/list-spells-classes-bard.md); [Cleric](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/lists/list-spells-classes-cleric.md); [Druid](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/lists/list-spells-classes-druid.md); [Paladin](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/lists/list-spells-classes-paladin.md)
