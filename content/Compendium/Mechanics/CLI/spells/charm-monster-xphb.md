# Charm Monster

_4th-level, Enchantment_

- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 30 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** 1 hour

One creature you can see within range makes a Wisdom saving throw. It does so with [Advantage](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/advantage-xphb.md) if you or your allies are fighting it. On a failed save, the target has the [Charmed](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/conditions.md#Charmed) condition until the spell ends or until you or your allies damage it. The [Charmed](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/conditions.md#Charmed) creature is [Friendly](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/friendly-attitude-xphb.md) to you. When the spell ends, the target knows it was [Charmed](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/conditions.md#Charmed) by you.

**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** You can target one additional creature for each spell slot level above 4.

**Classes**: [Bard](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/lists/list-spells-classes-bard.md); [Druid](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/lists/list-spells-classes-druid.md); [Fighter (Eldritch Knight)](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/lists/list-spells-classes-eldritch-knight-xphb.md "subclass=XPHB;class=XPHB"); [Rogue (Arcane Trickster)](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/lists/list-spells-classes-arcane-trickster-xphb.md "subclass=XPHB;class=XPHB"); [Sorcerer (Draconic Sorcery)](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/lists/list-spells-classes-draconic-sorcery-xphb.md "subclass=XPHB;class=XPHB"); [Sorcerer](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/lists/list-spells-classes-sorcerer.md); [Warlock](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/lists/list-spells-classes-warlock.md); [Wizard](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/lists/list-spells-classes-wizard.md)
