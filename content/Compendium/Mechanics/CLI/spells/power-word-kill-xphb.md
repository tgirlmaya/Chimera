# Power Word Kill

_9th-level, Enchantment_

- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You compel one creature you can see within range to die. If the target has 100 [Hit Points](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/hit-points-xphb.md) or fewer, it dies. Otherwise, it takes `12d12` Psychic damage.

**Classes**: [Bard](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/lists/list-spells-classes-bard.md); [Sorcerer](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/lists/list-spells-classes-sorcerer.md); [Warlock](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/lists/list-spells-classes-warlock.md); [Wizard](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/lists/list-spells-classes-wizard.md)

_Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 306. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_
