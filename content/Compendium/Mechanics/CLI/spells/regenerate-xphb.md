# Regenerate

_7th-level, Transmutation_

- **Casting time:** 1 minute
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S, M (a prayer wheel)
- **Duration:** 1 hour

A creature you touch regains `4d8 + 15` [Hit Points](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/hit-points-xphb.md). For the duration, the target regains 1 [Hit Point](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/hit-points-xphb.md) at the start of each of its turns, and any severed body parts regrow after 2 minutes.

**Classes**: [Bard](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/lists/list-spells-classes-bard.md); [Cleric](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/lists/list-spells-classes-cleric.md); [Druid](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/lists/list-spells-classes-druid.md)

_Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 311. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_
