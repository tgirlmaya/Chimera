# True Seeing

_6th-level, Divination_

- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S, M (mushroom powder worth 25+ GP, which the spell consumes)
- **Duration:** 1 hour

For the duration, the willing creature you touch has [Truesight](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/senses.md#Truesight) with a range of 120 feet.

**Classes**: [Bard](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/lists/list-spells-classes-bard.md); [Cleric](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/lists/list-spells-classes-cleric.md); [Sorcerer](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/lists/list-spells-classes-sorcerer.md); [Warlock](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/lists/list-spells-classes-warlock.md); [Wizard (Diviner)](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/lists/list-spells-classes-diviner-xphb.md "subclass=XPHB;class=XPHB"); [Wizard](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/lists/list-spells-classes-wizard.md)
