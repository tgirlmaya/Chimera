# Enthrall

_2nd-level, Enchantment_

- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** Concentration, up to 1 minute

You weave a distracting string of words, causing creatures of your choice that you can see within range to make a Wisdom saving throw. Any creature you or your companions are fighting automatically succeeds on this save. On a failed save, a target has a -10 penalty to Wisdom ([Perception](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/skills.md#Perception)) checks and Passive [Perception](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/skills.md#Perception) until the spell ends.

**Classes**: [Bard](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/lists/list-spells-classes-bard.md); [Warlock](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/lists/list-spells-classes-warlock.md)

_Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 269. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_
