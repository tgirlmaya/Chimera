# Foresight

_9th-level, Divination_

- **Casting time:** 1 minute
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S, M (a hummingbird feather)
- **Duration:** 8 hours

You touch a willing creature and bestow a limited ability to see into the immediate future. For the duration, the target has [Advantage](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/advantage-xphb.md) on [D20 Tests](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/d20-test-xphb.md), and other creatures have [Disadvantage](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/disadvantage-xphb.md) on attack rolls against it. The spell ends early if you cast it again.

**Classes**: [Bard](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/lists/list-spells-classes-bard.md); [Druid](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/lists/list-spells-classes-druid.md); [Warlock](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/lists/list-spells-classes-warlock.md); [Wizard (Diviner)](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/lists/list-spells-classes-diviner-xphb.md "subclass=XPHB;class=XPHB"); [Wizard](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/lists/list-spells-classes-wizard.md)
