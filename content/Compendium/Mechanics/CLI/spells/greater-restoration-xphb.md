# Greater Restoration

_5th-level, Abjuration_

- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** Touch
- **Components:** V, S, M (diamond dust worth 100+ GP, which the spell consumes)
- **Duration:** Instantaneous

You touch a creature and magically remove one of the following effects from it:

- 1 [Exhaustion](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/conditions.md#Exhaustion) level
- The [Charmed](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/conditions.md#Charmed) or [Petrified](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/conditions.md#Petrified) condition
- A curse, including the target's [Attunement](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/attunement-xphb.md) to a cursed magic item
- Any reduction to one of the target's ability scores
- Any reduction to the target's [Hit Point](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/hit-points-xphb.md) maximum

**Classes**: [Bard](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/lists/list-spells-classes-bard.md); [Cleric (Life Domain)](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/lists/list-spells-classes-life-domain-xphb.md "subclass=XPHB;class=XPHB"); [Cleric](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/lists/list-spells-classes-cleric.md); [Druid](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/lists/list-spells-classes-druid.md); [Paladin](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/lists/list-spells-classes-paladin.md); [Ranger](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/lists/list-spells-classes-ranger.md); [Sorcerer (Clockwork Sorcery)](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/lists/list-spells-classes-clockwork-sorcery-xphb.md "subclass=XPHB;class=XPHB"); [Warlock (Celestial Patron)](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/lists/list-spells-classes-celestial-patron-xphb.md "subclass=XPHB;class=XPHB")
