# [Knight](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/humanoid/knight-xmm.md)

_Source: Monster Manual (2024) p. 184. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_

Knights frequently lead troops in combat or work in units that dominate the battlefield. They're often attended by squires, who might be less skilled soldiers or commoners.

## Knights

_Battle Masters and Heroic Wanderers_

- **Habitat.** Any
- **Treasure.** [Armaments](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/tables/random-magic-items-armaments.md), Individual

Knights are skilled warriors trained for war and tested in battle. Many serve the rulers of a realm, a religion, or an order devoted to a cause.
