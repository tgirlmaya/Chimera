# [Questing Knight](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/humanoid/questing-knight-xmm.md)

_Source: Monster Manual (2024) p. 184_

Questing knights travel in pursuit of a cause, such as slaying a villain, defeating a monster, recovering an Artifact, or restoring their lost honor.

## Knights

_Battle Masters and Heroic Wanderers_

- **Habitat.** Any
- **Treasure.** [Armaments](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/tables/random-magic-items-armaments.md), Individual

Knights are skilled warriors trained for war and tested in battle. Many serve the rulers of a realm, a religion, or an order devoted to a cause.
