# Commanding Presence

_Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 95_

When you make a Charisma ([Intimidation](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/skills.md#Intimidation), [Performance](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/skills.md#Performance), or [Persuasion](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/skills.md#Persuasion)) check, you can expend one Superiority Die and add that die to the roll.
