# Devil's Sight

_Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 155. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span>_

**Prerequisite**: Level 2

You can see normally in [Dim Light](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/dim-light-xphb.md) and [Darkness](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/darkness-xphb.md)—both magical and nonmagical—within 120 feet of yourself.
