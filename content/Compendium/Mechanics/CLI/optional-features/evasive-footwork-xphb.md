# Evasive Footwork

_Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 95_

As a [Bonus Action](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/bonus-action-xphb.md), you can expend one Superiority Die and take the [Disengage](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/actions.md#Disengage) action. You also roll the die and add the number rolled to your AC until the start of your next turn.
