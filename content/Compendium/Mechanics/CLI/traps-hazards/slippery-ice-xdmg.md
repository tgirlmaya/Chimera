# Slippery Ice

_Environmental Hazard_

Slippery ice is [Difficult Terrain](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/difficult-terrain-xphb.md). A creature that moves onto slippery ice for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there must succeed on a DC 10 Dexterity saving throw or have the [Prone](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/conditions.md#Prone) condition.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 69_
