# Heavy Precipitation

_Environmental Hazard_

Everything within an area of heavy rain or heavy snowfall is [Lightly Obscured](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/lightly-obscured-xphb.md), and creatures in the area have [Disadvantage](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/disadvantage-xphb.md) on all Wisdom ([Perception](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/skills.md#Perception)) checks. Heavy rain also extinguishes open flames.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 69_
