# Ring of Feather Falling

_Ring, rare (requires attunement)_\
![](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/items/img/ring-of-feather-falling.webp#right)

When you fall while wearing this ring, you descend 60 feet per round and take no damage from falling.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 293. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_
