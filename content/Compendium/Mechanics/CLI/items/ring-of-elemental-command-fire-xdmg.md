# Ring of Elemental Command (Fire)

_Ring, legendary (requires attunement)_

The Ring of Elemental Command (fire) is linked to the Elemental Plane of Fire. Every Ring of Elemental Command has the following two properties:

- **Elemental Bane.** While wearing the ring, you have [Advantage](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/advantage-xphb.md) on attack rolls against Elementals and they have [Disadvantage](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/disadvantage-xphb.md) on attack rolls against you.
- **Elemental Compulsion.** While wearing the ring, you can take a [Magic](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/actions.md#Magic) action to try to compel an Elemental you see within 60 feet of yourself. The Elemental makes a DC 18 Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, the Elemental has the [Charmed](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/conditions.md#Charmed) condition until the start your next turn, and you determine what it does with its move and action on its next turn.

## Elemental Focus

While wearing the ring, you benefit from additional properties corresponding to the ring's linked Elemental Plane:

- **Fire.** You know Ignan, and you have [Immunity](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/immunity-xphb.md) to Fire damage.

## Spellcasting

The ring has 5 charges and regains `1d4 + 1` expended charges daily at dawn. While wearing the ring, you can cast a spell from it. A spell cast from the ring has a save DC of 18. Choose the spell from the following list: [Burning Hands](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/spells/burning-hands-xphb.md) (1 charge), [Fireball](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/spells/fireball-xphb.md) (2 charges), [Fire Storm](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/spells/fire-storm-xphb.md) (4 charges), [Wall of Fire](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/spells/wall-of-fire-xphb.md) (3 charges)

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 292. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_
