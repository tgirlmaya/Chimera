# Plate Armor of Etherealness

_Heavy armor, legendary (requires attunement)_

- **Armor Class**: 18
- **Strength**: Requires 15 STR.
- **Stealth**: The wearer has disadvantage on Stealth (DEX) checks.
- **Weight**: 65.0 lbs.

While you're wearing this armor, you can take a [Magic](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/actions.md#Magic) action and use a command word to gain the effect of the [Etherealness](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/spells/etherealness-xphb.md) spell. The spell ends immediately if you remove the armor or take a [Magic](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/actions.md#Magic) action to repeat the command word. This property of the armor can't be used again until the next dawn.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 286. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_
