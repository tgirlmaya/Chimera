# Ring of Jumping

_Ring, uncommon (requires attunement)_

While wearing this ring, you can cast [Jump](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/spells/jump-xphb.md) from it, but can target only yourself when you do so.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 293. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_
