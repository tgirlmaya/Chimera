# Cap of Water Breathing

_Wondrous item, uncommon_

While wearing this cap underwater, you can take a [Magic](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/actions.md#Magic) action to create a bubble of air around your head. This bubble allows you to breathe normally underwater. This bubble stays with you until the cap is removed or you are no longer underwater.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 242_
