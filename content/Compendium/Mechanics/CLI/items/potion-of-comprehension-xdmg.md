# Potion of Comprehension

_Potion, common_

- **Weight**: 0.5 lbs.

When you drink this potion, you gain the effect of the [Comprehend Languages](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/spells/comprehend-languages-xphb.md) spell for 1 hour.

This potion's liquid is a clear concoction with bits of salt and soot swirling in it.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 287_
