# Cloak of Billowing

_Wondrous item, common_\
![](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/items/img/cloak-of-billowing.webp#right)

While wearing this cloak, you can take a [Bonus Action](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/bonus-action-xphb.md) to make it billow dramatically for 1 minute.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 244_
