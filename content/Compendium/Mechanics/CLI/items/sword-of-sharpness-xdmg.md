# Sword of Sharpness

_Very rare (requires attunement)_\
![Sword of Sharpness (Greatsword)](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/items/img/sword-of-sharpness.webp#right)

When you attack an object with this magic weapon and hit, maximize your weapon damage dice against the target.

When you attack a creature with this weapon and roll a 20 on the `d20` for the attack roll, that target takes an extra 14 Slashing damage and gains 1 [Exhaustion](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/conditions.md#Exhaustion) level.

**Variants**:

- [Glaive of Sharpness](#Glaive%20of%20Sharpness)
- [Greatsword of Sharpness](#Greatsword%20of%20Sharpness)
- [Longsword of Sharpness](#Longsword%20of%20Sharpness)
- [Scimitar of Sharpness](#Scimitar%20of%20Sharpness)

- **Damage**: 1d10 slashing
- **Properties**: [Heavy](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/item-properties.md#Heavy), [Reach](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/item-properties.md#Reach), [Two-Handed](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/item-properties.md#Two-Handed)
- **Weight**: 6.0 lbs.

### Greatsword of Sharpness
