# Bell

_Adventuring gear_

- **Cost**: 1 gp

When rung as a [Utilize](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/actions.md#Utilize) action, a Bell produces a sound that can be heard up to 60 feet away.

_Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 224. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_
