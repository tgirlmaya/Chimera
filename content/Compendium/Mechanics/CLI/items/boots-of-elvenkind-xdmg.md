# Boots of Elvenkind

_Wondrous item, uncommon_\
![](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/items/img/boots-of-elvenkind.webp#right)

While you wear these boots, your steps make no sound, regardless of the surface you are moving across. You also have [Advantage](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/advantage-xphb.md) on Dexterity ([Stealth](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/skills.md#Stealth)) checks.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 239. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_
