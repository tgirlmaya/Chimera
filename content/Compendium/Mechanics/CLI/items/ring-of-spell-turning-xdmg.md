# Ring of Spell Turning

_Ring, legendary (requires attunement)_\
![](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/items/img/ring-of-spell-turning.webp#right)

While wearing this ring, you have [Advantage](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/advantage-xphb.md) on saving throws against spells. If you succeed on the save for a spell of level 7 or lower, the spell has no effect on you. If that spell targeted only you and didn't create an area of effect, you can take a [Reaction](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/reaction-xphb.md) to deflect the spell back at the spell's caster; the caster must make a saving throw against the spell using their own spell save DC.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 295. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_
