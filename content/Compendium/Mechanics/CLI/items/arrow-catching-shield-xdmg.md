# Arrow-Catching Shield

_Armor ([shield](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/items/shield-xphb.md)), rare (requires attunement)_

- **Armor Class**: 2
- **Weight**: 6.0 lbs.

You gain a +2 bonus to [Armor Class](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/armor-class-xphb.md) against ranged attack rolls while you wield this Shield. This bonus is in addition to the Shield's normal bonus to AC.

Whenever an attacker makes a ranged attack roll against a target within 5 feet of you, you can take a [Reaction](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/reaction-xphb.md) to become the target of the attack instead.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 231. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_
