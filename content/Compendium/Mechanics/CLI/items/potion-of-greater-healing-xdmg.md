# Potion of Greater Healing

_Potion, uncommon_

- **Weight**: 0.5 lbs.

You regain `4d4 + 4` [Hit Points](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/hit-points-xphb.md) when you drink this potion. The potion's red liquid glimmers when agitated.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 288. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_
