# Eyes of Minute Seeing

_Wondrous item, uncommon_

These crystal lenses fit over the eyes. While wearing them, your vision improves significantly out to a range of 1 foot, granting you [Darkvision](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/senses.md#Darkvision) within that range and [Advantage](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/advantage-xphb.md) on Intelligence ([Investigation](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/skills.md#Investigation)) checks made to examine something within that range.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 261. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_
