# Cloak of the Manta Ray

_Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement)_\
![](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/items/img/cloak-of-the-manta-ray.webp#right)

While wearing this cloak, you can breathe underwater, and you have a [Swim Speed](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/swim-speed-xphb.md) of 60 feet.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 245. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_
