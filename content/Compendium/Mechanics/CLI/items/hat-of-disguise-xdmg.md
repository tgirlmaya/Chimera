# Hat of Disguise

_Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement)_\
![](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/items/img/hat-of-disguise.webp#right)

While wearing this hat, you can cast the [Disguise Self](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/spells/disguise-self-xphb.md) spell. The spell ends if the hat is removed.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 266. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_
