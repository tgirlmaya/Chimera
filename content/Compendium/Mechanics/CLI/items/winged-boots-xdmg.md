# Winged Boots

_Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement)_\
![](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/items/img/winged-boots.webp#right)

These boots have 4 charges and regain `1d4` expended charges daily at dawn. While wearing the boots, you can take a [Magic](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/actions.md#Magic) action to expend 1 charge, gaining a [Fly Speed](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/fly-speed-xphb.md) of 30 feet for 1 hour. If you are flying when the duration expires, you descend at a rate of 30 feet per round until you land.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 325. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_
