# Malice

_Adventuring gear, poison (inhaled)_

- **Properties**: [Poison](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/item-properties.md#Poison)
- **Cost**: 250 gp

A creature subjected to Malice must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or have the [Poisoned](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/conditions.md#Poisoned) condition for 1 hour. The creature also has the [Blinded](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/conditions.md#Blinded) condition while [Poisoned](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/conditions.md#Poisoned) in this way.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 91. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span>_
