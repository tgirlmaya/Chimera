# Rations

_Adventuring gear_

- **Cost**: 5 sp
- **Weight**: 2.0 lbs.

Rations consist of travel-ready food, including jerky, dried fruit, hardtack, and nuts. See "[Malnutrition](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/traps-hazards/malnutrition-xphb.md)" for the risks of not eating.

_Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 228. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_
