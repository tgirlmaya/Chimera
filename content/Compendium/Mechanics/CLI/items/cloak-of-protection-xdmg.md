# Cloak of Protection

_Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement)_\
![](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/items/img/cloak-of-protection.webp#right)

You gain a +1 bonus to [Armor Class](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/armor-class-xphb.md) and saving throws while you wear this cloak.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 245. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_
