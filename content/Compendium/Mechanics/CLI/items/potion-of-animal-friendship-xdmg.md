# Potion of Animal Friendship

_Potion, uncommon_

- **Weight**: 0.5 lbs.

When you drink this potion, you can cast the level 3 version of the [Animal Friendship](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/spells/animal-friendship-xphb.md) spell (save DC 13).

Agitating this potion's muddy liquid brings little bits into view: a fish scale, a hummingbird feather, a cat claw, or a squirrel hair.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 287. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_
