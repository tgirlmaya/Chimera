# Brooch of Shielding

_Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement)_\
![](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/items/img/brooch-of-shielding.webp#right)

While wearing this brooch, you have [Resistance](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/resistance-xphb.md) to Force damage, and you have [Immunity](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/immunity-xphb.md) to damage from the [Magic Missile](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/spells/magic-missile-xphb.md) spell.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 241. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_
