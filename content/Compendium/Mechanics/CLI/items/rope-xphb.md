# Rope

_Adventuring gear_

- **Cost**: 1 gp
- **Weight**: 5.0 lbs.

As a [Utilize](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/actions.md#Utilize) action, you can tie a knot with Rope if you succeed on a DC 10 Dexterity ([Sleight of Hand](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/skills.md#Sleight%20of%20Hand)) check. The Rope can be burst with a successful DC 20 Strength ([Athletics](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/skills.md#Athletics)) check.

You can bind an unwilling creature with the Rope only if the creature has the [Grappled](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/conditions.md#Grappled), [Incapacitated](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/conditions.md#Incapacitated), or [Restrained](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/conditions.md#Restrained) condition. If the creature's legs are bound, the creature has the [Restrained](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/conditions.md#Restrained) condition until it escapes. Escaping the Rope requires the creature to make a successful DC 15 Dexterity ([Acrobatics](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/skills.md#Acrobatics)) check as an action.

_Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 228. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_
