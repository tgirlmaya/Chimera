# +3 Rod of the Pact Keeper

_Rod, very rare (requires attunement by a warlock)_

- **Weight**: 2.0 lbs.

While holding this rod, you gain a +3 bonus to spell attack rolls and to the saving throw DCs of your Warlock spells.

In addition, you can regain one spell slot as a [Magic](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/actions.md#Magic) action while holding the rod. You can't use this property again until you finish a [Long Rest](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/long-rest-xphb.md).

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 301_
