# Cloak of Elvenkind

_Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement)_\
![](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/items/img/cloak-of-elvenkind.webp#right)

While you wear this cloak, Wisdom ([Perception](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/skills.md#Perception)) checks made to perceive you have [Disadvantage](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/disadvantage-xphb.md), and you have [Advantage](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/advantage-xphb.md) on Dexterity ([Stealth](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/skills.md#Stealth)) checks.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 244. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_
