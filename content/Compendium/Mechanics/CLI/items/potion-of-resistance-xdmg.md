# Potion of Resistance

_Potion, uncommon_

- **Weight**: 0.5 lbs.

When you drink this potion, you have [Resistance](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/resistance-xphb.md) to one type of damage for 1 hour. The DM chooses the type or determines it randomly by rolling on the following table.
