# Quaal's Feather Token, Fan

_Wondrous item, uncommon_\
![](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/items/img/quaals-feather-token-fan.webp#right)

This object looks like a feather.

If you are on a boat or ship, you can take a [Magic](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/actions.md#Magic) action to toss the token up to 10 feet in the air. The token disappears, and a giant flapping fan takes its place. The fan floats and creates a strong wind. This wind can fill the sails of one ship, increasing its speed by 5 miles per hour for 8 hours. You can dismiss the fan as a [Magic](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/actions.md#Magic) action.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 290. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> (as "Feather Token, Fan") and the Free Rules (2024)_
