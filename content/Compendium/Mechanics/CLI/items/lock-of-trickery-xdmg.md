# Lock of Trickery

_Wondrous item, common_

This lock appears to be an ordinary [Lock](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/items/lock-xphb.md) (of the type described in chapter 6 of the "Player's Handbook") and comes with a single key. The tumblers in this lock magically adjust to thwart burglars. Dexterity checks made to pick the lock have [Disadvantage](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/disadvantage-xphb.md).

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 275_
