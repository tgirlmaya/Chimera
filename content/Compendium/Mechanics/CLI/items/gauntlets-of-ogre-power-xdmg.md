# Gauntlets of Ogre Power

_Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement)_\
![](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/items/img/gauntlets-of-ogre-power.webp#right)

Your Strength score is 19 while you wear these gauntlets. They have no effect on you if your Strength is 19 or higher without them.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 264. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_
