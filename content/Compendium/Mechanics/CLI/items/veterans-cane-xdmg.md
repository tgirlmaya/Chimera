# Veteran's Cane

_Wondrous item, common_

As a [Bonus Action](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/bonus-action-xphb.md), you can transform this walking cane into an ordinary Longsword or change the Longsword back into a walking cane. In either case, you must be holding the item.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 318_
