# Ring of Invisibility

_Ring, legendary (requires attunement)_\
![](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/items/img/ring-of-invisibility.webp#right)

While wearing this ring, you can take a [Magic](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/actions.md#Magic) action to give yourself the [Invisible](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/conditions.md#Invisible) condition. You remain [Invisible](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/conditions.md#Invisible) until the ring is removed or until you take a [Bonus Action](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/bonus-action-xphb.md) to become visible again.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 293. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_
