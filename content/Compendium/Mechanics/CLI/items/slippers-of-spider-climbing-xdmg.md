# Slippers of Spider Climbing

_Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement)_\
![](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/items/img/slippers-of-spider-climbing.webp#right)

While you wear these light shoes, you can move up, down, and across vertical surfaces and along ceilings, while leaving your hands free. You have a [Climb Speed](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/climb-speed-xphb.md) equal to your [Speed](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/speed-xphb.md). However, the slippers don't allow you to move this way on a slippery surface, such as one covered by ice or oil.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 304. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_
