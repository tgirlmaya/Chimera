# Bracers of Defense

_Wondrous item, rare (requires attunement)_\
![](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/items/img/bracers-of-defense.webp#right)

While wearing these bracers, you gain a +2 bonus to [Armor Class](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/armor-class-xphb.md) if you are wearing no armor and using no [Shield](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/items/shield-xphb.md).

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 241. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_
