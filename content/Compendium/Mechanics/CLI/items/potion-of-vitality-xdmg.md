# Potion of Vitality

_Potion, very rare_\
![](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/items/img/potion-of-vitality.webp#right)

- **Weight**: 0.5 lbs.

When you drink this potion, it removes any [Exhaustion](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/conditions.md#Exhaustion) levels you have and ends the [Poisoned](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/conditions.md#Poisoned) condition on you. For the next 24 hours, you regain the maximum number of [Hit Points](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/hit-points-xphb.md) for any [Hit Point Die](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/hit-point-dice-xphb.md) you spend.

This potion's crimson liquid regularly pulses with dull light, calling to mind a heartbeat.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 289. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_
