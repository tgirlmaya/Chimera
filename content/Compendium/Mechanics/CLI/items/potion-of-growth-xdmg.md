# Potion of Growth

_Potion, uncommon_

- **Weight**: 0.5 lbs.

When you drink this potion, you gain the "enlarge" effect of the [Enlarge/Reduce](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/spells/enlarge-reduce-xphb.md) spell for 10 minutes (no [Concentration](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/conditions.md#Concentration) required).

The red in the potion's liquid continuously expands from a tiny bead to color the clear liquid around it and then contracts. Shaking the bottle fails to interrupt this process.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 288. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_
