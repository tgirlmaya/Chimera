# Cloak of Arachnida

_Wondrous item, very rare (requires attunement)_

This fine garment is made of black silk interwoven with faint, silvery threads. While wearing it, you gain the following benefits.

## Poison Resistance

You have [Resistance](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/resistance-xphb.md) to Poison damage.

## Spider Climb

You have a [Climb Speed](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/climb-speed-xphb.md) equal to your [Speed](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/speed-xphb.md) and can move up, down, and across vertical surfaces and along ceilings, while leaving your hands free.

## Spider Walk

You can't be caught in webs of any sort and can move through webs as if they were [Difficult Terrain](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/difficult-terrain-xphb.md).

## Web

You can cast [Web](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/spells/web-xphb.md) (save DC 13). The web created by the spell fills twice its normal area. Once used, this property can't be used again until the next dawn.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 244. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_
