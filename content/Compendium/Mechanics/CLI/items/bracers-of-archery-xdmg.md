# Bracers of Archery

_Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement)_\
![](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/items/img/bracers-of-archery.webp#right)

While wearing these bracers, you have proficiency with the [Longbow](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/items/longbow-xphb.md) and [Shortbow](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/items/shortbow-xphb.md), and you gain a +2 bonus to damage rolls made with such weapons.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 240. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_
