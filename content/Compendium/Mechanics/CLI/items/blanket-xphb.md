# Blanket

_Adventuring gear_

- **Cost**: 5 sp
- **Weight**: 3.0 lbs.

While wrapped in a blanket, you have [Advantage](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/advantage-xphb.md) on saving throws against [extreme cold](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/traps-hazards/extreme-cold-xdmg.md) (see the "Dungeon Master's Guide").

_Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 224. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_
