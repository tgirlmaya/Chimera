# Potion of Greater Invisibility

_Potion, very rare_\
![](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/items/img/potion-of-greater-invisibility.webp#right)

- **Weight**: 0.5 lbs.

This potion's container looks empty but feels as though it holds liquid. When you drink the potion, you have the [Invisible](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/conditions.md#Invisible) condition for 1 hour.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 288_
