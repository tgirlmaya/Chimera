# Component Pouch

_Adventuring gear_

- **Cost**: 25 gp
- **Weight**: 2.0 lbs.

A Component Pouch is watertight and filled with compartments that hold all the free Material components of your spells.

_Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 225. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_
