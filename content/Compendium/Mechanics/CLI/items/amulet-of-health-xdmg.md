# Amulet of Health

_Wondrous item, rare (requires attunement)_\
![](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/items/img/amulet-of-health.webp#right)

- **Weight**: 1.0 lbs.

Your Constitution score is 19 while you wear this amulet. It has no effect on you if your Constitution is already 19 or higher without it.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 228. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_
