# Potion of Poison

_Potion, uncommon_

- **Weight**: 0.5 lbs.

This concoction looks, smells, and tastes like a [Potion of Healing](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/items/potion-of-healing-xdmg.md) or another beneficial potion. However, it is actually poison masked by illusion magic. [Identify](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/spells/identify-xphb.md) reveals its true nature.

If you drink this potion, you take `4d6` Poison damage and must succeed on a DC 13 Constitution saving throw or have the [Poisoned](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/conditions.md#Poisoned) condition for 1 hour.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 288. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_
