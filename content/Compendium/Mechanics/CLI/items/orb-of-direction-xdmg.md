# Orb of Direction

_Wondrous item, common_\
![](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/items/img/orb-of-direction.webp#right)

- **Weight**: 3.0 lbs.

This orb can be used as an Arcane Focus.

While holding this orb, you can take a [Magic](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/actions.md#Magic) action to determine which way is magnetic north. Nothing happens if the orb is used in a location that has no magnetic north.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 283_
