# Ring of Resistance

_Ring, rare_

You have [Resistance](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/resistance-xphb.md) to one damage type while wearing this ring. The gemstone in the ring indicates the type, which the DM chooses or determines randomly by rolling on the following table.
