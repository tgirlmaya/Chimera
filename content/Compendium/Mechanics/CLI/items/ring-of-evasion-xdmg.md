# Ring of Evasion

_Ring, rare (requires attunement)_\
![](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/items/img/ring-of-evasion.webp#right)

This ring has 3 charges, and it regains `1d3` expended charges daily at dawn. When you fail a Dexterity saving throw while wearing the ring, you can take a [Reaction](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/reaction-xphb.md) to expend 1 charge to succeed on that save instead.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 293. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_
