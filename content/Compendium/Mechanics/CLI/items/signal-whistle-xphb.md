# Signal Whistle

_Adventuring gear_

- **Cost**: 5 cp

When blown as a [Utilize](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/actions.md#Utilize) action, a Signal Whistle produces a sound that can be heard up to 600 feet away.

_Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 228. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_
