# Moon-Touched Sword

_Common_\
![Moon-Touched Sword (Rapier)](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/items/img/moon-touched-sword.webp#right)

In [Darkness](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/darkness-xphb.md), the unsheathed blade of this weapon sheds moonlight, creating [Bright Light](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/bright-light-xphb.md) in a 15-foot radius and [Dim Light](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/dim-light-xphb.md) for an additional 15 feet.
