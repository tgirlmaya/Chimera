# Costume

_Adventuring gear_

- **Cost**: 5 gp
- **Weight**: 4.0 lbs.

While wearing a Costume, you have [Advantage](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/advantage-xphb.md) on any ability check you make to impersonate the person or type of person it represents.

_Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 225. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_
