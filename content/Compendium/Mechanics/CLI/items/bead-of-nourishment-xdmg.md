# Bead of Nourishment

_Wondrous item, common_

This flavorless, gelatinous bead dissolves on your tongue and provides as much nourishment as 1 day of Rations.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 235. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_
