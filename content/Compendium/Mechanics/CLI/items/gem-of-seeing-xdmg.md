# Gem of Seeing

_Wondrous item, rare (requires attunement)_\
![](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/items/img/gem-of-seeing.webp#right)

This gem has 3 charges. As a [Magic](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/actions.md#Magic) action, you can expend 1 charge. For the next 10 minutes, you have [Truesight](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/senses.md#Truesight) out to 120 feet when you peer through the gem.

The gem regains `1d3` expended charges daily at dawn.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 264. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_
