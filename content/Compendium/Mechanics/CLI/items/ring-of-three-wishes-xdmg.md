# Ring of Three Wishes

_Ring, legendary_\
![](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/items/img/ring-of-three-wishes.webp#right)

While wearing this ring, you can expend 1 of its 3 charges to cast [Wish](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/spells/wish-xphb.md) from it. The ring becomes nonmagical when you use the last charge.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 296. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_
