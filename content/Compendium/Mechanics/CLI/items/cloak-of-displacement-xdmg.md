# Cloak of Displacement

_Wondrous item, rare (requires attunement)_\
![](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/items/img/cloak-of-displacement.webp#right)

While you wear this cloak, it magically projects an illusion that makes you appear to be standing in a place near your actual location, causing any creature to have [Disadvantage](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/disadvantage-xphb.md) on attack rolls against you. If you take damage, the property ceases to function until the start of your next turn. This property is suppressed while your [Speed](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/speed-xphb.md) is 0.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 244. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_
