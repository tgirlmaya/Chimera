# Circlet of Blasting

_Wondrous item, uncommon_\
![](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/items/img/circlet-of-blasting.webp#right)

While wearing this circlet, you can cast [Scorching Ray](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/spells/scorching-ray-xphb.md) with it (`+5` to hit). The circlet can't cast this spell again until the next dawn.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 244. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_
