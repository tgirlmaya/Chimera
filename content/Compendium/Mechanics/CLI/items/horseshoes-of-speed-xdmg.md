# Horseshoes of Speed

_Wondrous item, rare_

These horseshoes come in a set of four. As a [Magic](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/actions.md#Magic) action, you can touch one of the horseshoes to the hoof of a horse or similar creature, whereupon the horseshoe affixes itself to the hoof. Removing a horseshoe also takes a [Magic](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/actions.md#Magic) action.

While all four horseshoes are attached to the same creature, its [Speed](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/speed-xphb.md) is increased by 30 feet.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 270. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_
