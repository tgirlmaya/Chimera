# Pole of Angling

_Wondrous item, common_

This item functions as a Pole. While holding it, you can take a [Magic](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/actions.md#Magic) action to cause it to transform into a fishing pole with a hook, a line, and a reel, or have the fishing pole revert to a Pole.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 286_
