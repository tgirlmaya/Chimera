# Map

_Adventuring gear_

- **Cost**: 1 gp

If you consult an accurate Map, you gain a +5 bonus to Wisdom ([Survival](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/skills.md#Survival)) checks you make to find your way in the place represented on it.

_Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 227. Available in the Free Rules (2024)_
