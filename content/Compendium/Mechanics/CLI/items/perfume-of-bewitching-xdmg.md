# Perfume of Bewitching

_Wondrous item, common_

This tiny vial contains magic perfume, enough for one use. You can take a [Magic](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/actions.md#Magic) action to apply the perfume to yourself, and its effect lasts 1 hour. For the duration, you have [Advantage](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/advantage-xphb.md) on all Charisma ([Deception](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/skills.md#Deception) and [Persuasion](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/skills.md#Persuasion)) checks made to influence a creature within 5 feet of yourself.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 284_
