# Healer's Kit

_Adventuring gear_

- **Cost**: 5 gp
- **Weight**: 3.0 lbs.

A Healer's Kit has ten uses. As a [Utilize](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/actions.md#Utilize) action, you can expend one of its uses to stabilize an [Unconscious](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/conditions.md#Unconscious) creature that has 0 [Hit Points](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/hit-points-xphb.md) without needing to make a Wisdom ([Medicine](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/skills.md#Medicine)) check.

_Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 225. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_
