_Wondrous item, rare (requires attunement)_

While you wear these boots, you can cast [Levitate](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/spells/levitate-xphb.md) on yourself.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 239. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_
