# Wings of Flying

_Wondrous item, rare (requires attunement)_\
![](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/items/img/wings-of-flying.webp#right)

While wearing this cloak, you can take a [Magic](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/actions.md#Magic) action to turn the cloak into a pair of wings on your back. The wings lasts for 1 hour or until you end the effect early as a [Magic](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/actions.md#Magic) action. The wings give you a [Fly Speed](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/fly-speed-xphb.md) of 60 feet. If you are aloft when the wings disappear, you fall. When the wings disappear, you can't use them again for `1d12` hours.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 324. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_
