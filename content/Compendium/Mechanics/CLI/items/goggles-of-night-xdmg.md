# Goggles of Night

_Wondrous item, uncommon_\
![](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/items/img/goggles-of-night.webp#right)

While wearing these dark lenses, you have [Darkvision](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/senses.md#Darkvision) out to 60 feet. If you already have [Darkvision](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/senses.md#Darkvision), wearing the goggles increases its range by 60 feet.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 265. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_
