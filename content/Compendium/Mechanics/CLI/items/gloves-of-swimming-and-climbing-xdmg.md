# Gloves of Swimming and Climbing

_Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement)_

While wearing these gloves, climbing and swimming don't cost you extra movement, and you gain a +5 bonus to Strength ([Athletics](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/skills.md#Athletics)) checks made to climb or swim.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 265. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_
