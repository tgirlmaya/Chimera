# Helm of Telepathy

_Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement)_\
![](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/items/img/helm-of-telepathy.webp#right)

While wearing this helm, you have telepathy with a range of 30 feet, and you can cast [Detect Thoughts](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/spells/detect-thoughts-xphb.md) or [Suggestion](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/spells/suggestion-xphb.md) (save DC 13) from the helm. Once either spell is cast from the helm, that spell can't be cast from it again until the next dawn.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 268. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_
