# Ring of Water Walking

_Ring, uncommon_\
![](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/items/img/ring-of-water-walking.webp#right)

While wearing this ring, you cast [Water Walk](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/spells/water-walk-xphb.md) from it, targeting only yourself.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 296. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_
