# +2 Wand of the War Mage

_Wand, rare (requires attunement by a spellcaster)_

- **Weight**: 1.0 lbs.

While holding this wand, you gain a +2 bonus to spell attack rolls. In addition, you ignore [Half Cover](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/cover-xphb.md) when making a spell attack roll.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 322. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_
