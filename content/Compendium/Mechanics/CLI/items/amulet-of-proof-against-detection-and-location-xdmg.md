# Amulet of Proof against Detection and Location

_Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement)_\
![](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/items/img/amulet-of-proof-against-detection-and-location.webp#right)

- **Weight**: 1.0 lbs.

While wearing this amulet, you can't be targeted by Divination spells or perceived through magical scrying sensors unless you allow it.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 228. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_
