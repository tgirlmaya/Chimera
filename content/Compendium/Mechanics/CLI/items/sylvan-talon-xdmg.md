# Sylvan Talon

_Common (requires attunement)_\
![Sylvan Talon (Scimitar)](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/items/img/sylvan-talon.webp#right)

While this weapon is on your person, you understand the non-written communication of all Fey, and they understand yours.

## Secret Message

As a [Magic](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/actions.md#Magic) action, you can use the weapon to cast [Message](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/spells/message-xphb.md). Once this property is used, it can't be used again until the next dawn.
