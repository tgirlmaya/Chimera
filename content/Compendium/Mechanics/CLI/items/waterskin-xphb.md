# Waterskin

_Adventuring gear_

- **Cost**: 2 sp
- **Weight**: 5.0 lbs.

A Waterskin holds up to 4 pints. If you don't drink sufficient water, you risk dehydration.

_Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 229. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_
