# Military Saddle

_Tack and harness_

- **Cost**: 20 gp
- **Weight**: 30.0 lbs.

A saddle comes with a bit, a bridle, reins, and any other equipment needed to use the saddle. A Military Saddle gives [Advantage](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/advantage-xphb.md) on any ability check you make to remain mounted.

_Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 230. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_
