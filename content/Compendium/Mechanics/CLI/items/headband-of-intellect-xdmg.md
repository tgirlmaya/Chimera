# Headband of Intellect

_Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement)_\
![](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/items/img/headband-of-intellect.webp#right)

Your Intelligence score is 19 while you wear this headband. It has no effect on you if your Intelligence is 19 or higher without it.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 268. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_
