# Prosthetic Limb

_Wondrous item, common_\
![](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/items/img/prosthetic-limb.webp#right)

This magic item replaces a lost limb—a hand, an arm, a foot, a leg, or a similar body part. While the prosthetic is attached, it functions identically to the part it replaces. You can detach or reattach it as a [Magic](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/actions.md#Magic) action, and it can't be removed against your will while you are alive.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 290_
