# Silver Dragon Scale Mail

_Medium armor ([scale mail](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/items/scale-mail-xphb.md)), very rare (requires attunement)_\
![](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/items/img/dragon-scale-mail.webp#right)

- **Armor Class**: 14 + Dex modifier (max of +2)
- **Stealth**: The wearer has disadvantage on Stealth (DEX) checks.
- **Weight**: 45.0 lbs.

Dragon Scale Mail is made of the scales of one kind of dragon. Sometimes dragons collect their cast-off scales and gift them. Other times, hunters carefully preserve the hide of a dead dragon. In either case, Dragon Scale Mail is highly valued.

While wearing this armor, you gain a +1 bonus to [Armor Class](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/armor-class-xphb.md), you have [Advantage](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/advantage-xphb.md) on saving throws against the breath weapons of Dragons, and you have [Resistance](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/resistance-xphb.md) to cold damage.

Additionally, you can focus your senses as a [Magic](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/actions.md#Magic) action to discern the distance and direction to the closest silver dragon within 30 miles of yourself. This action can't be used again until the next dawn.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 254. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_
