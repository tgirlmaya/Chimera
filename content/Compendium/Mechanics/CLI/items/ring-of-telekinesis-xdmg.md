# Ring of Telekinesis

_Ring, very rare (requires attunement)_\

While wearing this ring, you can cast [Telekinesis](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/spells/telekinesis-xphb.md) from it.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 295. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_
