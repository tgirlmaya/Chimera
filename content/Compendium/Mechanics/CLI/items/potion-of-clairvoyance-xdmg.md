# Potion of Clairvoyance

_Potion, rare_

- **Weight**: 0.5 lbs.

When you drink this potion, you gain the effect of the [Clairvoyance](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/spells/clairvoyance-xphb.md) spell (no [Concentration](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/conditions.md#Concentration) required).

An eyeball bobs in this potion's yellowish liquid but vanishes when the potion is opened.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 287. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_
