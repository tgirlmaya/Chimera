# Stone of Good Luck

_Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement)_\
![](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/items/img/stone-of-good-luck-luckstone.webp#right)

While this polished agate is on your person, you gain a +1 bonus to ability checks and saving throws.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 312. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_
