# Periapt of Health

_Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement)_\
![](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/items/img/periapt-of-health.webp#right)

- **Weight**: 1.0 lbs.

While wearing this pendant, you can take a [Magic](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/actions.md#Magic) action to regain `2d4 + 2` [Hit Points](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/hit-points-xphb.md). Once used, this property can't be used again until the next dawn.

In addition, you have [Advantage](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/advantage-xphb.md) on saving throws to avoid or end the [Poisoned](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/conditions.md#Poisoned) condition while you wear this pendant.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 284. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_
