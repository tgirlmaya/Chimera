# Quaal's Feather Token, Bird

_Wondrous item, rare_\
![](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/items/img/quaals-feather-token-bird.webp#right)

This object looks like a feather.

You can take a [Magic](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/actions.md#Magic) action to toss the token 5 feet into the air. The token disappears and an enormous, multicolored bird takes its place. The bird has the statistics of a [Roc](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/bestiary/monstrosity/roc-xmm.md), but it can't attack. It obeys your simple commands and can carry up to 500 pounds while flying at its maximum speed (16 miles per hour for a maximum of 144 miles per day, with a 1-hour rest for every 3 hours of flying) or 1,000 pounds at half that speed. The bird disappears after flying its maximum distance for a day or if it drops to 0 [Hit Points](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/hit-points-xphb.md). You can dismiss the bird as a [Magic](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/actions.md#Magic) action.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 290. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> (as "Feather Token, Bird") and the Free Rules (2024)_
