# Bullseye Lantern

_Adventuring gear_

- **Cost**: 10 gp
- **Weight**: 2.0 lbs.

A Bullseye Lantern burns Oil as fuel to cast [Bright Light](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/bright-light-xphb.md) in a 60-foot Cone and [Dim Light](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/dim-light-xphb.md) for an additional 60 feet.

_Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 226. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_
