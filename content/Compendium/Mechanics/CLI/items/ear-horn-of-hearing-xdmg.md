# Ear Horn of Hearing

_Wondrous item, common_

While held up to your ear, this horn suppresses the effects of the [Deafened](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/conditions.md#Deafened) condition on you.

_Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 256_
