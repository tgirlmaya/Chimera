# Boon of the Night Spirit

_Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 211. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)_

**Prerequisite**: 19th

You gain the following benefits.

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase one ability score of your choice by 1, to a maximum of 30.

**Merge with Shadows.** While within [Dim Light](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/dim-light-xphb.md) or [Darkness](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/darkness-xphb.md), you can give yourself the [Invisible](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/conditions.md#Invisible) condition as a [Bonus Action](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/bonus-action-xphb.md). The condition ends on you immediately after you take an action, a [Bonus Action](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/bonus-action-xphb.md), or a [Reaction](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/reaction-xphb.md).

**Shadowy Form.** While within [Dim Light](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/dim-light-xphb.md) or [Darkness](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/darkness-xphb.md), you have [Resistance](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/resistance-xphb.md) to all damage except Psychic and Radiant.
