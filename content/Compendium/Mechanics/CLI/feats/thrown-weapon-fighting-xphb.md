# Thrown Weapon Fighting

_Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 210_

**Prerequisite**: Fighting Style Feature

When you hit with a ranged attack roll using a weapon that has the [Thrown](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/item-properties.md#Thrown) property, you gain a +2 bonus to the damage roll.
