# Boon of Skill

_Source: Player's Handbook (2024) p. 211_

**Prerequisite**: 19th

You gain the following benefits.

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase one ability score of your choice by 1, to a maximum of 30.

**All-Around Adept.** You gain proficiency in all skills.

**Expertise.** Choose one skill in which you lack [Expertise](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/expertise-xphb.md). You gain [Expertise](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/expertise-xphb.md) in that skill.
