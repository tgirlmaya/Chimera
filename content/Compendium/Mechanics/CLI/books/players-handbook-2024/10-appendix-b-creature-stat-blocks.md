# Appendix B: Creature Stat Blocks

_Source: Player's Handbook (2024), p. 346_

This appendix provides stat blocks for creatures mentioned elsewhere in the book, particularly in the class, equipment, and spell chapters. See the "rules glossary" for how to read a [stat block](Compendium/Mechanics/CLI/Rules/variant-rules/stat-block-xphb.md), and see the _Monster Manual_ for even more creatures.
