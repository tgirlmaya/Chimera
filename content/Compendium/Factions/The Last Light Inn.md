## Overview

The Last Light Inn considers themselves the last bastion of hope in the shadow-cursed [[Compounds]]. They are a guild of heroes dedicated to protecting those passing through and ultimately lifting the Shadow Curse from the land.

## Relations

- Four Corners - Enemy
- New Age Gnomes - Neutral
- College of the Giants - Neutral
- Half-Fractured - Neutral
- Castle of Order - Friendly
- [[The Chosen Rifted]] - Ally

## History

**~1465**
After the Last Light Inn was formed, they needed a
'base of operations'. The Shadow was not able to be lit by any non-magical means, so they consulted the Castle of Order for help. The Castle was able to send a Wizard to assist in their forming, and invented a type of magical lantern able to nullify the Shadow Curse's magic.
**1488**
[[The Chosen Rifted]] arrive at the Inn to assist in lifting the Curse. Siveril comes to their aid and supplies weapons and resources. [[The Chosen Rifted]] end up lifting the Shadow Curse from the [[Compounds]].
**~1500**
The Last Light Inn is recognized by the sector as a safe haven.
