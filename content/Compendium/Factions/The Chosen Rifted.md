## Overview

The Chosen Rifted are two unlikely adventurers transported to Chimera through the Four Corners' Rift. They were originally sent to cleanse the Compounds of the Shadow by Agathyus, but ended up bringing peace to Chimera through liberating the Wild Sectors.
